🎬 Robo Cranes Video Kit

The best VEX videos to watch, plus 6 short teaching videos to make: scripts, shot lists and copy-ready prompts for Runway or Higgsfield.

How to make each video (about 20 minutes each)

  1. Generate the shots. In Runway or Higgsfield, use image-to-video: upload the shot's reference image, paste its prompt, pick 5 seconds. Make 2 versions and keep the better one.
  2. Record the voiceover yourself on your phone, reading the script slowly. Kids listen better to a real voice they know, and you can record a Spanish version too. An AI voice also works.
  3. Edit in CapCut or iMovie: put the clips in order, add the voice, then add big captions for the key words ("1 point", "3 points", "6 points!").

Rules that keep it looking good: no words inside the generated clips (AI garbles text, so add words when you edit) · one simple motion per clip · always start from these reference images so Cranebot and Flop look the same in every video · keep each video under 60 seconds.

The images and Cranebot are original drawings made for this class, so you're free to use them. VEX's own photos and videos are copyrighted: link to them, don't feed them into a generator. The game facts in the scripts come from VEX's Level Up resources; the full game has higher pyramid levels that the scripts leave out for 3rd graders.

Meet Flop, Our Robot

45 sec · use in Session 1

Voiceover script

Hi Robo Cranes! I'm Cranebot, and this is Flop, our robot! Flop has five parts, just like a body. Wheels are Flop's legs: they drive and turn. The intake is Flop's mouth: it grabs bean bags and spits them out. The arm is Flop's neck: it lifts bags up high, just like my long crane neck! The camera is Flop's eyes: it sees red, blue and yellow. And the bumper is Flop's nose: boop! Five parts, one robot, one team. Let's build it!

How Points Work

50 sec · use in Session 1

Voiceover script

How do we score in Level Up? With bean bags! Drop a bean bag in the floor goal: one point. Put it on top of the pyramid: three points! Now here's the secret. Put a bag ON TOP of another bag on the pyramid, and it reaches the next level: six points! That's double! So the best teams drop every bag in the exact same spot, so they stack. One, three, six. Higher means more points!

Walk and Chew Gum: Wait vs. Don't Wait

45 sec · use in Session 6

Voiceover script

Flop can do things one at a time: drive... stop... then lift the arm. That's slow! Or Flop can do two things at once: lift the arm WHILE it drives. In VEXcode, click the little arrow on the arm block and pick 'and don't wait.' Now the next block starts right away. It's like walking and chewing gum at the same time. Save a second here, a second there, and you'll have time for more bean bags!

The Lost Robot: Turn to Heading

45 sec · use in Session 6

Voiceover script

Every time Flop turns, it can be a tiny bit wrong. One little mistake is okay. But after many turns, the mistakes add up, and Flop gets lost! The fix: instead of 'turn right 90,' use 'turn to heading.' That means 'face that direction,' like 'face the window.' Flop checks its compass every time, so mistakes don't pile up. Smart robot!
