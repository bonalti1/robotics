Human Robot Challenge 🧍‍♂️🤖

Put this map on the projector. Set up the floor to match, then the Coder gives exact commands to the Robot.

Allowed words only:
  • "Forward ___ steps"
  • "Turn left" / "Turn right"
  • "Grab" / "Drop"
  • "Arm up" / "Arm down" (hands up or down!)

⬛ Barrier = a jump rope held low. Robots must duck (arm down) to get under it!
